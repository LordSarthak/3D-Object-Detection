Turn overlapping photos into a scaled 3D model.

Capture a stationary object from different angles, then build a mesh you can open in Blender or another 3D tool. This workflow needs COLMAP installed on this computer; results are not a metrology-grade scan.

Before you build

  1. 1Use a textured, matte objectGlossy, transparent, plain, moving, or reflective objects are difficult to reconstruct.
  2. 2Keep overlap between photosTake 8–30 sharp views around the whole stationary object. Keep the background visible.
  3. 3Measure the longest dimensionUse a ruler or caliper. The final model is uniformly scaled to this measurement.

3 · Reconstruct and export

Centimeters. The saved mesh will use meters. Enter a real measurement for dimensional scale.

Reality check

Scale is set from the longest model extent and the dimension you enter. Missing photo overlap, hidden surfaces, or reconstruction outliers can distort both shape and dimensions. Inspect the exported mesh before using it for manufacturing, fit, or safety decisions.

Photos stay on this computer in the app's scan_sessions folder until you clear the scan. The first object-identification request also runs on the local Flask server.
